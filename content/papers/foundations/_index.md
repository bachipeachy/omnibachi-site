These papers are prior disclosure. They are DOI-published, permanently citable, and
frozen: they are cited for what they established when they were written and are not
revised to match the current architecture, because normalizing them would destroy the
priority record.

The PGC papers come first, then the field manual, then the earlier PGS papers in
reading order.
