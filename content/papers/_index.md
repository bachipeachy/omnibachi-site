**[Preprints](/papers/preprints/)** — papers under review, each deposited under its own DOI
and each asking a question no other paper in the set asks.

**[Published](/papers/published/)** — papers accepted in a peer-reviewed journal, carrying
the DOI of the Version of Record.

**[Foundations](/papers/foundations/)** — the deposited PGC papers and field manual, the
earlier PGS papers they build on, and the historic working papers a named successor has
replaced. Frozen as prior disclosure.
