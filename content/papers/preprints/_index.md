Most papers here are deposited under their own DOI; the IEEE and SoSyM papers are not. Most are
under review at a named journal; the table says where each stands.
The page you read is the **Author's Original Version** — the version completed before
submission — and is the citable preprint until the Version of Record appears.

| Paper | Publisher | Status | Asks about |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Who Authorizes Software Behavior?](/papers/preprints/who-authorizes-software-behavior/) | IEEE *Computer* | under review | the problem |
| [Autonomy Without Authority](/papers/preprints/autonomy-without-authority/) | Springer *Automated Software Engineering* | under review | the agent |
| [A Software Development Lifecycle Architecture](/papers/preprints/lifecycle-architecture/) | — | not accepted (JSS, then SCP); being reworked | the standard |
| [Architectural Inversion](/papers/preprints/architectural-inversion/) | Wiley *Software: Practice and Experience* | under review | the consequences |
| [Business Software on a Domain-Neutral Substrate](/papers/preprints/domain-neutral-substrate/) | Oxford University Press *The Computer Journal* | under review | the substrate |
| [Behavioral Authority in Sealed Models](/papers/preprints/behavioral-authority/) | Springer *Software and Systems Modeling* | under review | the semantics |

They rest on one architecture and one reference realization, and each asks a question
that appears in no other paper as a question. Where one paper's result is
needed by another it enters as a cited premise or as positioning, never as a restated
contribution.
