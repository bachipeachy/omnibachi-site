Each paper here is deposited under its own DOI and is under review at a named journal.
The page you read is the **Author's Original Version** — the version completed before
submission — and is the citable preprint until the Version of Record appears.

| Paper | Publisher | Asks about |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Who Authorizes Software Behavior?](/papers/preprints/who-authorizes-software-behavior/) | IEEE *Computer* | the problem |
| [Autonomy Without Authority](/papers/preprints/autonomy-without-authority/) | Springer *Automated Software Engineering* | the agent |
| [A Software Development Lifecycle Architecture](/papers/preprints/lifecycle-architecture/) | Elsevier *Journal of Systems and Software* | the standard |
| [Architectural Inversion](/papers/preprints/architectural-inversion/) | Wiley *Software: Practice and Experience* | the consequences |
| [Business Software on a Domain-Neutral Substrate](/papers/preprints/domain-neutral-substrate/) | Oxford University Press *The Computer Journal* | the substrate |

They rest on one architecture and one reference realization, and each asks a question
that appears in no other paper as a question. Where one paper's result is
needed by another it enters as a cited premise or as positioning, never as a restated
contribution.
